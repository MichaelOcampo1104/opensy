# OReilly2019

**Purpose:** Quasi-static cyclic pushover of a 3D non-ductile RC frame representative of Italian pre-1970s construction (gravity-only design).

**Building System:** Model of a 2/3 scale RC frame designed for gravity loads only and tested by Calvi et al. (2003). Reinforced concrete frames typical of Italy (1950s–1970s), designed for gravity loads only. Masonry infill provides additional stiffness and strength. Uses smooth bars with insufficient detailing and anchorage.

**Model Description:** Model of a 3-story, 3-bay moment-resisting frame. Custom OpenSees elements used for walls and joints.
